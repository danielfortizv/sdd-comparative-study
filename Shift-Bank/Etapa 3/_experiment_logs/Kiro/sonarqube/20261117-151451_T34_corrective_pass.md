### C) typescript:S8786 — non-linear regex backtracking (1/1)

- **`frontend/src/components/AccountsPanel.tsx:14`** (`formatCop`): replaced the thousands-separator
  regex `/\B(?=(\d{3})+(?!\d))/g` (ambiguous nested quantifier, super-linear) with the anchored,
  equivalent form `/\d(?=(?:\d{3})+$)/g` and replacement `"$&."`. The `$` anchor removes the

  from the backend decimal), output is identical (e.g. `"1234567"` → `"1.234.567"`), so the
  displayed `COP …,dec` formatting is byte-identical.
